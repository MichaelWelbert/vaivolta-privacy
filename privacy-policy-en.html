VaiVolta Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the VaiVolta app handles information used to track customers, returnable items, inventory, and transactions. Michael Welbert is responsible for the app.

1. Information handled by the app

VaiVolta may handle:

  • the business name entered by the user;
  • the name, phone number, address, type, and notes of registered customers;
  • the selected type of returnable item and, when “Other” is selected, the name entered by the user;
  • quantities, balances, inventory records, and transactions for those items;
  • information needed to verify Premium subscription status.

Items may be water jugs, beverage crates, gas cylinders, pallets, or another returnable item defined by the user. The app does not require a VaiVolta account.

2. How information is used

This information is used to organize customers, calculate balances and inventory, record deliveries, returns, and adjustments, generate reports, and enable Premium features.

3. Local storage and Android backup

Business records are stored locally on the device. The person responsible for VaiVolta does not receive or access these records through a server of their own.

If Android backup is enabled for the user's Google account, the operating system may include app data in a backup and restore it to another device. The user controls this feature, and Google operates it according to the account settings and its policies.

4. Exporting and sharing

VaiVolta can generate PDF or CSV reports. These files are sent to another app or recipient only when the user chooses to share them. After sharing, the file is also subject to the practices of the selected app or recipient.

5. Subscriptions and payments

Premium subscriptions are offered and processed through Google Play. Google may handle the account and payment information needed to complete and manage a purchase. VaiVolta receives only the information needed to verify the purchase and enable the subscribed features; the app does not receive full payment card details.

6. Ads, analytics, and sale of data

VaiVolta does not display ads, include its own tracking or behavioral analytics tools, or sell personal information.

7. Retention and deletion

Records remain on the device while the user keeps them. Records can be edited or deleted within the app. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings deletes the local copy. Any backup copies follow the controls and retention periods of the user's Google account.

8. Security

VaiVolta uses the security features provided by Android for local storage. No storage method is completely risk-free, so we recommend protecting the device and keeping its operating system up to date.

9. Intended audience

VaiVolta is intended for managing business activities and is not directed at children.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the app or legal requirements. The latest update date will always appear at the top of the page.

11. Contact

For questions, requests, or privacy matters, contact Michael Welbert at michael.welbert.contato@gmail.com.
