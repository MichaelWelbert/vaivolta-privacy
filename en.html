VaiVolta Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

PortuguêsEnglishEspañol

This Privacy Policy explains how VaiVolta handles information used to manage customers, water containers, inventory and transactions. Michael Welbert is responsible for the application.

1. Information handled by the application

VaiVolta handles information entered by users themselves, which may include:

  • business name;
  • customer name, phone number, address, type and notes;
  • inventory records, water container balances and transactions;
  • information about Premium plan status.

The application does not require its own VaiVolta account.

2. Purpose of processing

Information supports the functions requested by users, such as organizing customers, calculating inventory, recording transactions, generating reports and enabling Premium plan features.

3. Local storage and Android backup

Business data is stored locally on the device. The person responsible for VaiVolta does not receive or access this data through their own server.

If Android backup is enabled in the user’s Google account, the operating system may include app data in a backup and restore it on another device. This feature is controlled by the user and operated by Google according to account settings and Google’s policies.

4. Export and sharing

VaiVolta can generate files such as PDF or CSV reports. Files are sent to another app or recipient only when the user chooses to export and share the content. After sharing, the file is also subject to the practices of the chosen app or recipient.

5. Subscriptions and payments

Premium subscriptions are offered and processed through Google Play. Google may handle account and payment data needed to complete and manage the purchase. VaiVolta receives only the information needed to verify the purchase and enable the contracted features; the application does not receive complete card details.

6. Advertising, analytics and sale of data

VaiVolta does not display advertisements, include its own tracking or behavioral analytics tools, or sell personal information.

7. Retention and deletion

Records remain on the device while the user keeps them. Records can be edited or deleted within the app. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings deletes the local copy. Any backup copies follow the controls and retention periods of the user’s Google account.

8. Security

VaiVolta uses Android’s security features for local storage. No storage method is entirely risk-free; we recommend protecting your device and keeping the operating system updated.

9. Application audience

VaiVolta is intended to manage business activities and is not directed at children.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the application or legal requirements. The date of the latest version is always shown at the top of the page.

11. Contact

For questions, requests or privacy-related matters, contact Michael Welbert by email at michael.welbert.contato@gmail.com.
